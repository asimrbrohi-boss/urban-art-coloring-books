# 25-Page Book Plan

## Concept
This 25-page coloring book follows a contemporary urban-art theme: playful city animals, cartoon scenes, and simple educational pages with bold line art suitable for children.

1. Front cover concept page (not counted in interior final if using separate cover)
2. Title page
3. City cat on a skateboard
4. Dog riding a bike
5. Fox with graffiti-style leaves
6. Bear carrying a picnic basket in the park
7. Parrot on a city rooftop
8. Rabbit at the neighborhood market
9. Turtle crossing a city street with caution signs
10. Panda on a bus stop bench
11. Hippo playing music near a mural wall
12. Lion reading a map in the city square
13. Elephant delivering books by bicycle
14. Frog in a raincoat near puddles and trees
15. Penguin on a roller skate path
16. Zebra waving from a bus window
17. Learning shapes: circles, squares, triangles
18. Learning numbers 1–10 with city objects
19. Learning letters A–Z in playful blocks
20. City road scene with cars, buses, and signs
21. Park scene with benches, trees, and birds
22. Street mural wall with geometric patterns
23. Community helper scene: firefighter, doctor, teacher, builder
24. Neighborhood map scene with shops, park, and school
25. Back cover concept page

## Notes for execution
- Keep the art simple enough for young children to color without frustration.
- Use bold black outlines and fewer tiny details.
- Mix educational pages with fun scenes to broaden appeal.
- Make urban elements feel friendly and safe rather than gritty or harsh.
- Use consistent character design across pages to create a recognizable series look.

## Recommended page style
Each page should feel like a single visual moment with:
- 1 main character
- 1 clear scene idea
- 2–3 supporting objects
- large open spaces for coloring
- minimal background clutter
