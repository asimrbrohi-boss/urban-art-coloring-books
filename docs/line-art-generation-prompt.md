# AI Line-Art Prompt

Use this prompt to generate printable, child-friendly line-art pages for the urban art coloring book series.

Prompt:

Create a black-and-white coloring page for children ages 4–8. Use a friendly urban-art style with bold, clean outlines and no shading. The page should feature a playful city animal or child-friendly urban scene in a modern cartoon aesthetic. Include simple, safe, and fun elements like skateboards, bikes, buses, market stalls, park benches, rooftops, tree-lined streets, murals, and neighborhood objects. The overall style should be less fairytale and more modern street-art energy, but still gentle and highly colorable. Keep the composition open and uncluttered, with large positive space and thick black contours suitable for coloring. Avoid complex details, tiny lines, or overly dark scary imagery. Make the design print-ready and suitable for a 8.5 x 11 coloring book page.

## Optional variations
- Add a city cat skateboarding on a sidewalk ramp
- Draw a dog riding a bicycle through a neighborhood
- Add a parrot on a rooftop with city skyline
- Show a bear at a market with fruits and crates
- Create an educational page with numbers or letters integrated into city objects

## Output guidance
- Output in black and white only
- Use crisp, consistent line thickness
- Include no gradients or grayscale fills
- Keep the figure centered and clear
- Ensure the final product works as a printable coloring book page
