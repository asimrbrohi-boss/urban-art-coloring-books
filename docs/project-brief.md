# Urban Art Coloring Book Project Brief

## Product concept
This project creates a series of children's coloring books with strong urban and street-art energy, but still safely child-friendly and playful. The visual style is energetic, modern, and expressive, with cartoon animals, city scenes, friendly vehicles, and educational pages designed for young children.

## Target audience
- Children ages 4–8
- Parents buying educational, screen-free activities
- Gift buyers looking for creative, fun, and non-fairy-tale kids' books

## Book format
- Trim size: 8.5 x 11 inches
- Interior: black-and-white line art
- Page count: 25 pages
- Binding: standard paperback coloring book
- Orientation: portrait

## Visual direction
The style should feel like:
- Bold outlines
- Clean shapes
- Friendly city characters
- Urban objects: buses, bikes, ladders, crates, murals, sidewalks, parks, rooftops, street signs, trees
- Animals with city personality, not fantasy creatures
- Less fairytale, more real-world fun and urban creativity

## Core themes
1. Animals in the city
2. Cartoon urban scenes
3. Educational city and community pages
4. Friendly transportation
5. Colorable murals and characters
6. Neighborhood adventure stories

## Recommended titles
- Urban Jungle Coloring Book
- City Creatures Coloring Book
- Street Art Safari Coloring Book
- Little City Explorers Coloring Book
- Cool Cats & City Streets Coloring Book

## Publishing approach
- Build a small series around a consistent style
- Use a single KDP-friendly interior format
- Keep page art clean and printable at high resolution
- Export final file as PDF for KDP print
- Add a cover with a bold urban/graphic aesthetic

## Design standards
- 300 DPI or higher for print-ready assets
- Pure black line art on white background
- Avoid too much detail in tiny areas
- Leave enough margins for safe print
- Keep each page easy to color for children

## Success criteria
The finished book should feel:
- Bright and fun
- Easy for children to color
- Distinct from fantasy-heavy coloring books
- Attractive to parents seeking educational and creative activity books
- Strong enough to support a series of related titles
