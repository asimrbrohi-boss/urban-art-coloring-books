# Urban Jungle Coloring Book

A modern, child-friendly coloring book series for Amazon KDP with bold urban-art energy, city animals, neighborhood adventures, and simple educational fun.

## Recommended series name
Urban Jungle Coloring Book

## Recommended first title
Urban Jungle Coloring Book: City Animals and Neighborhood Adventures

## Why this works
- Friendly and memorable
- Feels modern, urban, and playful
- Clearly distinct from fantasy-heavy coloring books
- Easy to expand into a series

## Format
- Trim size: 8.5 x 11 inches
- Interior: black-and-white line art
- Page count: 25 pages
- Audience: ages 4–8
- Style: bold, modern, city-inspired, child-safe

## Book concept
This first book follows city animals doing fun, everyday things in a neighborhood setting: skateboarding, biking, reading maps, riding buses, visiting the market, playing in the park, and exploring a mural-lined street.

## Brand direction
- Urban and playful
- Clean, friendly shapes
- Safe, bright, not gritty
- Street-art inspired without being too aggressive
- Distinct from fantasy or fairytale themes

## Suggested follow-up titles in the same series
- City Creatures Coloring Book
- Street Art Safari Coloring Book
- Little City Explorers Coloring Book
- Cool Cats & City Streets Coloring Book
