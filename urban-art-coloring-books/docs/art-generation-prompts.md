# Art Generation Prompt Pack

## Series master prompt
Create a black-and-white coloring page for children ages 4–8, using a modern urban-art style. Use bold, clean outlines and simple shapes. Include friendly city animals in playful neighborhood scenes with objects like skateboards, bikes, buses, murals, rooftops, park benches, market stalls, street signs, trees, and city skylines. Keep the style child-friendly, safe, and energetic, with less fantasy and more modern street-art energy. The composition should be open and uncluttered, with large spaces for coloring and no shading or grayscale.

## Cover prompt
Create a black-and-white children’s coloring book cover featuring a friendly city cat skateboarding in the foreground, with a cheerful neighborhood scene behind including a city skyline, a mural wall, trees, and street details. Use bold clean outlines, modern urban-art style, thick lettering for “Urban Jungle Coloring Book” and subtitle “City Animals and Neighborhood Adventures,” with readable text and a white background.

## Back cover prompt
Create a black-and-white coloring book back cover featuring 3 to 4 friendly city animals together in a neighborhood scene, with a mural wall, bus stop, park bench, trees, and simple city details. Use bold clean lines, playful composition, and modern urban-art styling. Include short descriptive text about the book in a clean, easy-to-read layout.

## Production rules
- Use only black and white
- Keep lines thick and consistent
- No grayscale, gradients, or shading
- Make the main subject clear and centered
- Include generous white space around the subject
- Avoid tiny details that frustrate young children
- Keep each page easy to color and print clearly at 8.5 x 11
