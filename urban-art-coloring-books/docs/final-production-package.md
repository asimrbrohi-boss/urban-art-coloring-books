# Final Production Package

## Cover hero choice
Final hero character: city cat on a skateboard

## Cover design brief
Front cover:
- A friendly city cat in the foreground, skating on a sidewalk ramp
- The cat has a cheerful expression and simple, rounded facial features
- Background includes a mural wall, city skyline, a few trees, and subtle neighborhood details
- Title: Urban Jungle Coloring Book
- Subtitle: City Animals and Neighborhood Adventures
- Typography: bold, rounded, modern, child-friendly lettering
- Style: clean black outlines, white background, urban street-art energy
- Overall look: polished, friendly, energetic, not dark or gritty

Back cover:
- 3–4 small friendly city animals interacting in a neighborhood scene
- Mural wall, park bench, bus stop, trees, and city pathway
- Keep the composition open and easy to color
- Include short marketing copy describing the book as a fun neighborhood adventure for children

## KDP template outline
- Page size: 8.5 x 11 inches
- Portrait orientation
- 25 pages interior
- Full bleed optional, but safe margins strongly recommended
- White background with black ink only
- High resolution for print safety
- Each page has one central subject and enough white space for coloring

## Production workflow
1. Create cover mockup files
2. Produce interior page art in batches of five
3. Review consistency in character shapes, line weight, and scene style
4. Export final PDF for KDP
5. Prepare metadata and upload to Amazon KDP
