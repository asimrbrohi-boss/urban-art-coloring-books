# Urban Jungle Coloring Book – First Production Batch

## Book title
Urban Jungle Coloring Book: City Animals and Neighborhood Adventures

## Cover mockup brief
Main subject: city cat skateboarding in the foreground
Background: city skyline, mural wall, simple trees, sidewalk ramp, and neighborhood details
Title: Urban Jungle Coloring Book
Subtitle: City Animals and Neighborhood Adventures
Style: modern urban-art, bold lines, white background, black ink only
Tone: friendly, fun, energetic, child-safe

## Page 1 concept
City cat skateboarding on sidewalk with street signs and urban details.

## Page 2 concept
Dog riding a bike through a neighborhood street with trees and a bike lane.

## Page 3 concept
Fox beside a mural wall with geometric graffiti-inspired patterns.

## Page 4 concept
Bear carrying a picnic basket in a park with a bench and trees.

## Page 5 concept
Parrot perched on a rooftop with a city skyline behind.

## Production checklist
- Use thick black outlines
- Keep background simple and uncluttered
- Center the main subject on the page
- Use large negative space for coloring
- No grayscale or shading
- Make all characters friendly and expressive
- Ensure all artwork prints cleanly at 8.5 x 11
