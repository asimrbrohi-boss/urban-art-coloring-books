# Production Plan for Urban Jungle Coloring Book

## Book title
Urban Jungle Coloring Book: City Animals and Neighborhood Adventures

## Series name
Urban Jungle Coloring Book

## Format
- Trim size: 8.5 x 11 inches
- Interior: black-and-white line art
- Page count: 25 pages
- Audience: ages 4–8
- Binding: standard KDP paperback coloring book
- Orientation: portrait

## Cover concept
### Front cover
- Main character: a playful city cat or dog riding a skateboard or bike
- Background: friendly city skyline, trees, street signs, and a mural wall
- Title: Urban Jungle Coloring Book
- Subtitle: City Animals and Neighborhood Adventures
- Style: bold, rounded lettering, clean black outlines, white background
- Tone: modern and cheerful, not dark or gritty

### Back cover
- Smaller scene featuring 3–4 city animals in a neighborhood setting
- Short descriptive text about the book
- Minimal but playful graphic design

### Visual identity
- Thick, easy-to-color outlines
- Friendly animal expressions
- Urban objects: bicycles, skateboards, buses, crates, park benches, trees, market stalls, rooftops
- Clean composition with large open areas for children to color

## Design rules for each page
- One main subject per page
- One clear scene idea
- Large open spaces for coloring
- Thick black outlines
- Avoid clutter and tiny details
- Keep the scene friendly and safe
- Make every page easy for ages 4–8 to color without frustration

## KDP production checklist
- Create a full black-and-white PDF for the interior
- Use high-resolution artwork for print safety
- Keep all pages in portrait orientation
- Ensure margins are clean and readable
- Export a print-ready PDF without web artifacts
- Prepare separate cover files for front and back cover
- Review all page layouts before uploading

## Recommended next actions
1. Finalize the cover hero character
2. Prepare the front and back cover concept mockups
3. Produce the full 25-page interior art set
4. Export the final KDP-ready PDF
5. Upload to Amazon KDP and prepare metadata
