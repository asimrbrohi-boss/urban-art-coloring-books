# Series Brand and Title Plan

## Final recommendation
Series name: Urban Jungle Coloring Book

Primary title for Book 1: Urban Jungle Coloring Book: City Animals and Neighborhood Adventures

## Why this is the best choice
- The phrase “Urban Jungle” is memorable and strong.
- It clearly signals the combination of city life and animal characters.
- It is modern and marketable for Amazon search.
- It gives room for future books in the same style.

## Visual brand direction
- Bold, rounded forms
- Urban objects like skateboards, bikes, buses, trees, fences, and mural walls
- Friendly animals with expressive faces
- High-contrast line art for easy coloring
- A playful street-art energy without harsh or dark visuals

## Cover concept direction
- Large animal character in the foreground, such as a cat, dog, or parrot
- City skyline or neighborhood backdrop
- Simple but punchy title text
- Thick outlines and a clean graphic treatment
- White background with black ink and a modern layout

## Series expansion ideas
Book 2: City Creatures Coloring Book
Book 3: Little City Explorers Coloring Book
Book 4: Street Art Safari Coloring Book

## Tiered page themes
Each book can rotate between the following themes:
- City animal scenes
- Neighborhood activities
- Transportation and road scenes
- Park and community areas
- Learning pages with letters, numbers, and shapes
- Murals and street-art details

## Production guidance
- Keep the series consistent in style and structure
- Use the same title treatment and color usage across all books
- Build a recognizable look so customers can spot the series quickly
- Keep all interior pages simple, bright, and print-safe
